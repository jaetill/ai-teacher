feat(templates): teacher-defined lesson templates, derived from her own lessons

Until now the shape of a lesson was a decision made in our code:
lessons.lesson_plan held exactly one thing, {activities: [...]}, and the
generation prompt hardcoded that key. This makes the shape data the
teacher owns.

A template is an ordered list of fields - stable key, her label, text or
list, required or not, and one line telling the AI what belongs there.
Named and reusable rather than one-per-course, because her own lesson
titles already show at least two shapes (a reading day and a Socratic
seminar), and forcing a seminar through a reading day's fields would
make the consistency report lie.

The first thing /templates offers is not a blank form but "suggest from
my lessons": derive reads a sample of what she has already written and
proposes the structure she is already using, which she then edits.
Recognition beats recall, and it never writes to her curriculum - it
returns a proposal. Too few lessons, or an unusable model response,
falls back to a conventional starter and says so rather than dressing a
guess up as analysis.

Two things follow from the template being data:
- generation asks for her fields (templateToPromptSchema) instead of
  ours, so imported lessons arrive in her structure;
- the report answers "which lessons don't match how I say I teach",
  with the most-often-missing fields called out.

Back-compat needs no backfill. Resolution is lesson -> course -> her
default -> the Classic builtin, every step nullable, and Classic's
single "activities" list is exactly the shape every existing lesson
already has. Deleting a template detaches courses and lessons rather
than cascading into content.

Also fixes stale copy on the calendar footer: meeting days moved to
Manage sections in #676, but the footer still pointed at the course
calendar.

Tests: 46 new (template rules, owner scoping incl. builtins being
unwritable, derive fallbacks, delete detaching). Suite 610 green.
